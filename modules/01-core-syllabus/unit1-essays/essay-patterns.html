Clave:
• Block: Análisis completo de A antes de B.
• Alternating: Comparación punto por punto (ABAB).
• Criteria: Deben definirse en la introducción.
• Domino: Estructura de causa-efecto secuencial.

Complete the following statements using the article's terminology:

According to the authors, the most important element of the introduction is the t__ s_____, which contains the idea of the whole essay and shows the attitude of the author to the topic.

The first sentence in a body paragraph is called the t__ s_____, which identifies the main idea of the paragraph.

The final sentence in a body paragraph can either return the reader to the topic or act as a c__ s__ ___ linking to the next paragraph.

Respuestas: thesis statement, topic sentence, concluding sentence.
Tip: Muchos estudiantes confunden el topic sentence con la tesis. Aclarar que el topic sentence es solo para un párrafo, mientras que la tesis es para todo el ensayo.
