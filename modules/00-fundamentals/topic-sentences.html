The Remote Control

What is it?

Imagine your paragraph is a TV. The Topic Sentence is the remote control. It tells the reader exactly which "channel" (specific idea) they are going to watch.

Key Fact: It is usually the first sentence. It must be clear, specific, and avoid being overly broad.

Identifying the Components

Drag the fragments to build a professional Topic Sentence:

"Social media"
"has negatively impacted the attention span of teenagers."
Clave:
• Topic: "Social media" (el sujeto).
• Controlling Idea: "has negatively impacted..." (lo que se va a probar sobre el sujeto).
Tip: Sin una controlling idea, el topic sentence es solo un título, no una oración temática.

Avoiding the "Kitchen Sink"

Identify the error: 'Modern architecture is beautiful, expensive, uses glass, and helps the environment.'

A. Clean Focus
B. Kitchen Sink Error (Too many ideas)
Respuesta: B — Kitchen Sink Error.
Tip: "Beautiful", "expensive", "uses glass" y "helps the environment" son 4 canales diferentes. Un párrafo solo puede tratar uno de ellos a fondo.

Practice Time

Which sentence is too broad for a single paragraph?

A. Global warming is a complex problem with international solutions.
B. One immediate effect of global warming is rising sea levels in coastal cities.
Respuesta: A — Global warming is a complex problem...
Tip: La opción A es un libro entero, no un párrafo. La opción B es perfecta porque se enfoca en un solo efecto (sea levels) en un lugar específico (coastal cities).

Apply It: Write the Missing Topic Sentence

Paragraph draft (topic sentence removed):

"___________. Employees who work from home report saving an average of ninety minutes a day that would otherwise go to commuting. Many also describe lower stress levels, since they avoid rush-hour traffic and can start the workday calmer. Some companies have even documented a small rise in output among fully remote staff compared to office-based teams."

Write the missing topic sentence (topic + controlling idea) that could introduce all three supporting details above without simply listing "time, stress, and productivity" as three separate claims (avoid the Kitchen Sink error). Then, in 1-2 sentences, explain why your controlling idea passes the "So What?" test — why does it matter, not just describe an observable fact?

La topic sentence tiene un topic claro ("remote work" / "working from home") y una controlling idea única que puede englobar los tres detalles (tiempo, estrés, productividad) sin enumerarlos como afirmaciones separadas (Kitchen Sink).
La explicación identifica correctamente por qué la controlling idea responde al "So What?" — es decir, por qué el beneficio del trabajo remoto es significativo y no solo un hecho observable.

Mastered?

Select the most effective academic Topic Sentence:

A. In this paragraph, I will talk about video games.
B. Playing strategic video games can significantly improve problem-solving skills.
Respuesta: B.
Tip: Nunca empieces un párrafo anunciando "En este párrafo hablaré de...". Ve directamente a la afirmación.
