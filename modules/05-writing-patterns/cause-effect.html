Cause and Effect

Three expository patterns organise much of the academic prose that neither argues nor narrates a problem: cause and effect (why does something happen?), classification (what types exist?) and process (how, and in what order?). This lesson covers the first. The next two lessons cover the other two.

The model

This sentence comes from the energy-poverty article you met in the SPRE lesson. Read it looking for the word that connects the cause with its effects.

This phenomenon poses a major barrier towards achieving the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and addressing climate change, since lack of access to reliable energy generation systems hinders the development of economic activities and limits the comfort of living conditions.
Eustachio, J. H. P. P. (2024). Addressing the challenges posed by energy poverty in Latin American countries. Discover Sustainability, 5, Article 262. https://doi.org/10.1007/s43621-024-00426-9

Taking it apart

Label each segment: Effect, Signal (the connector) or Cause.

This phenomenon poses a major barrier towards achieving the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and addressing climate change,
since
lack of access to reliable energy generation systems
hinders the development of economic activities and limits the comfort of living conditions.
Libro: «“since” conecta la causa (falta de acceso a energía) con dos efectos encadenados; frena la actividad económica y limita el confort».

Why this works

Each pattern answers a different reader question: cause and effect answers why does it happen?, classification answers what types are there?, process answers how, and in what order?.

Confusing the pattern a text needs is as serious as omitting a move: a reader who expects a classification and receives a causal chain loses the thread, even if every sentence is well written.

Anclaje: elegir el patrón correcto es una decisión retórica previa a escribir.

Rebuild it: order the causal chain

These four sentences form a cause-and-effect chain from a hypothetical article on water pollution. Put them in order, from the first cause to the last effect.

Untreated wastewater from the processing industry is discharged directly into the estuary.
As a result of this discharge, the concentration of heavy metals in the sediments has risen.
This contamination accumulates in the organisms that feed on the sediment.
Consequently, the fishing communities that depend on those organisms face a health risk.
Ejercicio de práctica con oraciones construidas. Observar que cada oración retoma el efecto anterior como causa de la siguiente (dado/nuevo, Cap. 2).

Rebuild it: causal connectors

Choose the connector that expresses the cause-effect relationship in each sentence.

since due to as a result of however for example in contrast

The harvest declined [ ? ] irregular rainfall over three consecutive seasons.

Prices rose sharply [ ? ] imported inputs became more expensive.

[ ? ] the policy change, enrolment increased in rural schools.

Phrase bank del Cap. 12: “X leads to Y, since Z.”, “This is due to…”, “As a result of X, Y occurs.”

Spot it in the wild

Find a short passage in an open-access article from your field that explains why something happens.

  1. Does the passage explain why something happens, what types there are, or how something was done step by step?
  2. Is the signal a single word (since, due to) or a complete structure (leads to, as a result of)?

Not graded. Bring your answers to class and compare them with a classmate.

Phrase bank: cause and effect

Save the phrases you want to reuse. They go to your personal notebook.

Now you write

You will write your own paragraph using the Cause and effect pattern. Allow about 10 minutes. Your writing is tracked for academic integrity: write it yourself, in your own words.

Write in English a short cause-and-effect paragraph of three to five sentences about a topic from your own field. Use a single pattern; do not mix it with classification or process.

Self-assessment rubric: Cause and effect

Before you submit, check your paragraph against each row. If three or more rows need work, rewrite before you hand it in.

CriterionAchievedNeeds work
I chose one pattern; I did not mix cause and effect with classification in the same paragraph.
I use at least one signal typical of cause and effect (since, due to, as a result of, leads to).
The cause-effect chain is logical: each effect follows from its cause.
A reader who reads only my first sentence can anticipate that this is a cause-effect paragraph.
