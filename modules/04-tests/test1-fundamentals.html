Phase 2 — Advanced Academic Writing & Integration

Proceed to write your 4 body paragraphs applying ALL of the following criteria:

  • PEER Model + One Point Rule: Each paragraph must follow Point → Evidence → Explanation → Relevance, focused on a single variable subordinate to the thesis.
  • Cause Structure (×1 minimum): Use cause-oriented connectors (e.g., because, since, due to the fact that, as a result of).
  • Effect Structure (×1 minimum): Use effect-oriented connectors (e.g., therefore, consequently, as a result, this leads to).
  • Formal Register: Absolutely prohibited: thing, bad, good, stuff, a lot, get, make, do, have (as weak verbs). Substitute with AWL equivalents.
  • AWL Integration: Include at least 5 distinct words from the Academic Word List organically woven into your paragraphs.
