Module 05: Academic Writing Patterns

Academic Writing Patterns

A Practical Guide for EFL University Students

Dr. Arturo Rodríguez Zambrano, PhD · July 2026 · work in progress (unpublished manuscript)

Academic English is more predictable than it looks. Behind articles, essays and reports there is a small repertoire of structures that repeat. In this module you learn each pattern from a real, published passage: you take it apart, see why it works, rebuild it and write your own.

Every lesson follows the same seven steps: the model, taking it apart, why this works, spot the pattern, rebuild it, now you write, phrase bank.
