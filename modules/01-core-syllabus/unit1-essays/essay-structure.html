The Keyhole Model & Rhetorical Coherence

Pay attention to your instructor's explanation of the Keyhole Model. We will use this framework to analyze the research paper.

Funnel Introduction
Thesis Statement
Inverted Funnel
Body Paragraphs
Moves from broad generalizations to a specific thesis statement.
The 'soul' of the writing, usually placed at the end of the intro.
The conclusion, which broadens and embellishes the thesis.
Act like 'little essays' with their own beginnings and endings.
Clave:
• Funnel: De lo general a lo específico (Tesis).
• Thesis: El 'alma' del escrito.
• Inverted Funnel: La conclusión (amplía la tesis).
• Body Paragraphs: Mini-ensayos con su propia estructura.
Tip: Usar la analogía del "ojo de la cerradura" (Keyhole) para visualizar cómo el ensayo se cierra en la tesis y se abre en la conclusión.
