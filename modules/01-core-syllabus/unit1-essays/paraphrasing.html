Introduction

Paraphrasing: Same Idea, New Words

A paraphrase restates one idea in your own words and your own sentence structure. It is about the same length as the original (a summary is much shorter) and it always needs a citation.

1. Change the structure — reorder ideas, or switch active ↔ passive
2. Change the vocabulary — precise academic synonyms
3. Change the word class — reduce (verb) → a reduction (noun)

Warning: swapping a few synonyms and keeping the same structure is patchwriting. It still counts as plagiarism.

Enfoque: práctica sobre teoría. El pptx original lista 6 métodos; aquí se agrupan en 3 técnicas trabajables (estructura, vocabulario, categoría gramatical) y la sexta (citar) tiene su propio slide. Paráfrasis ≈ misma longitud que el original.

Always Credit the Author

A paraphrase uses your words, but the idea belongs to the author. Two ways to cite (APA 7):

Author in the sentence:
Herrera (2018) argues that regular exercise lowers the risk of illness.

Author in parentheses:
Regular exercise lowers the risk of illness (Herrera, 2018).

Useful reporting verbs: argues, notes, explains, suggests, claims, reports, highlights.

Nota: el corpus de práctica usa autores ficticios creados para el curso; en trabajos reales el estudiante debe citar la fuente verdadera. Verbos de reporte: claims es más fuerte que suggests; elegir según qué tan firme es la afirmación del autor.

Real Paraphrase or Not?

Original: "Regular physical exercise significantly reduces the risk of developing chronic diseases in adults." (Herrera, 2018)
Drag each student version to the right category.

Adults who exercise often are much less likely to develop long-term illnesses (Herrera, 2018).
Regular physical exercise greatly lowers the risk of developing chronic diseases in adults (Herrera, 2018).
Regular physical exercise significantly reduces the risk of developing chronic diseases in adults.
According to Herrera (2018), the likelihood of long-term illness drops considerably for adults who stay physically active.
Clave:
• Patchwriting: misma estructura, solo cambió significantly→greatly y reduces→lowers. Aunque cite, no es paráfrasis válida.
• Plagio: copia literal sin autor ni año.
• Válidas: cambian estructura y vocabulario y citan.

Build It #1: Exercise and Health

Original (fictional source written for this course): Herrera (2018)

"Regular physical exercise significantly reduces the risk of developing chronic diseases, such as diabetes and cardiovascular conditions, in adults of all ages."

Step 1 — Change the structure. Rewrite the sentence starting with a different part, for example "Adults of all ages who..." or "The risk of chronic diseases...". Keep the meaning.

Regular exercise lowers the risk of chronic diseases (diabetes, heart/cardiovascular problems).
This applies to adults of all ages.
Conserva el significado: el ejercicio regular reduce el riesgo de enfermedades crónicas (p. ej. diabetes o cardiovasculares).
Cambia la estructura de la oración (orden de las ideas o voz) en lugar de solo reemplazar palabras, y mantiene "adultos de todas las edades".

Step 2 — Change the vocabulary. Now improve your sentence: replace the key words with academic synonyms (exercise, reduces, risk, chronic diseases). Edit the text above.

Regular exercise lowers the risk of chronic diseases (diabetes, heart problems) in adults of all ages.
Reemplaza las palabras clave por sinónimos precisos y académicos (p. ej. physical activity, decreases/lowers, likelihood, long-term illnesses) sin cambiar el significado.
El significado sigue fiel al original y el texto suena natural y gramaticalmente correcto.

Step 3 — Credit the author. Add the citation: Herrera (2018) in the sentence, or (Herrera, 2018) at the end.

Regular exercise lowers the risk of chronic diseases in adults of all ages.
Incluye una cita correcta en formato APA: Herrera (2018) o (Herrera, 2018).
La paráfrasis final conserva el significado del original y no copia frases literales.

Build It #2: Time Management

Original (fictional source written for this course): Salazar (2021)

"Because many first-year students lack effective time-management strategies, they often underestimate how long academic tasks take, which leads to rushed and incomplete assignments."

Step 1 — Change the structure. Reorder the ideas (for example, start with the result) or change the grammar. Keep the meaning.

First-year students lack good time-management strategies.
They underestimate how long tasks take.
The result is rushed / incomplete assignments.
Conserva las tres ideas: falta de estrategias de gestión del tiempo, subestimar cuánto tardan las tareas y trabajos apresurados/incompletos.
La estructura es claramente distinta de la original (orden o gramática), no solo sinónimos.

Step 2 — Change the vocabulary. Improve the wording: use different words for lack, strategies, underestimate, rushed. Consider changing a word class (verb → noun).

First-year students lack good time-management strategies; they underestimate task duration; assignments end up rushed or incomplete.
Sustituye vocabulario clave con equivalentes precisos (p. ej. new students, planning skills, misjudge, hurried) o cambia la categoría gramatical.
El significado es fiel a las tres ideas del original y la oración es gramaticalmente correcta.

Step 3 — Credit the author. Add the citation and use a reporting verb (notes, argues, explains...).

First-year students lack time-management skills, underestimate task duration, and submit rushed/incomplete work.
Cita a Salazar (2021) en formato APA y usa un verbo de reporte (notes, argues, explains, etc.).
La paráfrasis final es fiel, con estructura y vocabulario propios.

Build It #3: Digital Tools and Focus

Original (fictional source written for this course): Alvarado and Ponce (2022)

"Although digital tools have expanded access to information, researchers have warned that constant connectivity may weaken students' ability to concentrate on demanding texts."

Step 1. Paraphrase the sentence with a different structure.

Digital tools have expanded access to information.
Constant connectivity may weaken students' ability to concentrate on demanding texts.
Conserva ambas ideas: las herramientas digitales amplían el acceso a la información y la conectividad constante puede debilitar la concentración en textos exigentes.
Cambia la estructura (p. ej. voz pasiva, o comenzar por la advertencia) de forma clara.

Step 2. Now change the vocabulary and at least one word class.

Digital tools have expanded access to information; constant connectivity may weaken concentration on demanding texts.
Usa vocabulario académico distinto al original y al menos un cambio de categoría gramatical.
Mantiene el significado, incluido el matiz de posibilidad (may / could) sin exagerarlo.

Step 3. Add the citation for two authors (APA 7: Alvarado and Ponce (2022) or (Alvarado & Ponce, 2022)).

Digital tools have expanded access to information; constant connectivity may weaken concentration on demanding texts.
Cita a los dos autores correctamente en APA 7 (Alvarado and Ponce, 2022 / Alvarado & Ponce, 2022).
La paráfrasis final es fiel, natural y no copia frases literales.
