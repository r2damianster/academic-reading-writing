Cause & Effect Connectors

Two Directions, One Relationship

Every cause-effect connector points in one of two directions. Getting the direction wrong inverts the logic of your sentence entirely.

→ Introduces the EFFECT (what happened as a result):
therefore / thus / hence / consequently / as a result / for this reason

← Introduces the CAUSE (why it happened):
because / since / as / due to / owing to / as a consequence of

Test: after writing the sentence, ask "Does my connector point toward the cause or the effect?" If the answer doesn't match the direction of the sentence, swap families.

Which Direction?

Choose the connector that points in the correct logical direction.

The glacier has retreated by 40% over the past decade owing to therefore rising average temperatures in the region.

Average temperatures in the region have risen significantly; because consequently , glacial retreat has accelerated beyond previous projections.

The clinical trial was halted due to thus unexpected adverse effects in a subset of participants.

Respuestas:
1. owing to: El aumento de temperatura es la CAUSA del retroceso del glaciar (nota: "as a result of" no es correcto aquí porque la familia "as a result" está clasificada como introductora de EFECTO en esta lección; "owing to" es inequívocamente de la familia CAUSA y toma una frase sustantiva).
2. consequently: El retroceso acelerado es el EFECTO del aumento de temperatura.
3. due to: Los efectos adversos son la CAUSA de que se detuviera el ensayo clínico.

Subtle Differences Within Each Family

Not all connectors in the same family are interchangeable. Register and grammar matter:

since / as — imply the cause is already known to the reader.
"Since the budget was cut, the project was delayed." (reader knows about the budget cut)

because — introduces a cause that may be new information.
"The project was delayed because an unexpected audit revealed compliance issues."
hence / thus — very formal; common in academic papers and legal writing.
therefore — formal and neutral; the safest choice in most academic contexts.
so — informal; avoid in academic essays.
due to / owing to — followed by a noun or noun phrase, never a full clause.
❌ "Due to the temperature rose..."
✅ "Due to the rise in temperature..."

Fine-Tuning the Choice

Since Because the committee had already reviewed the report, no further discussion was required.

The laboratory was evacuated due to because a gas leak detected in the ventilation system.

Participation rates fell sharply last semester. So Therefore , the faculty has introduced mandatory attendance tracking.

Respuestas:
1. Since: Se asume que el lector ya sabe que el comité revisó el reporte.
2. due to: Va seguido de una frase sustantiva ("a gas leak...").
3. Therefore: Evita el So por ser informal en este contexto académico.

Wrong Direction

One sentence contains a cause-effect connector pointing in the wrong logical direction. Click on it.

Urban air pollution has reached critical levels in several major cities. This is largely due to the rapid expansion of private vehicle use over the past two decades. Therefore the number of diesel vehicles on the road increased, respiratory disease rates among urban populations rose by 22%. As a result, several city governments have introduced low-emission zones and congestion charges. Since these measures have proven effective in comparable European cities, their adoption is likely to expand.
Respuesta: "Therefore the number of diesel vehicles..."
Tip: El aumento de vehículos diesel es la CAUSA, pero Therefore introduce un EFECTO. El conector correcto sería Because o As.

Apply It: Which Way Does It Point?

Excerpt from an internal incident report:

"On March 3, the payment gateway rejected roughly 2,300 transactions over a 45-minute window. Therefore, the on-call engineer had deployed a configuration change to the rate-limiter twenty minutes earlier without running it through staging first."

This report gets the direction backwards: "Therefore" introduces what is actually the CAUSE of the outage, not its effect. Rewrite the second sentence using a connector from the correct family and direction, keeping both concrete details — the 2,300 rejected transactions and the rate-limiter change made twenty minutes earlier. Then, in one sentence, name which family "Therefore" actually belongs to, and explain why it fails here.

Reescribe la oración usando un conector de la familia CAUSA (because, since, as, due to, owing to) colocado correctamente para introducir el cambio en el rate-limiter como la causa de los 2,300 rechazos, respetando la gramática de la familia elegida (p. ej. "due to"/"owing to" + frase sustantiva, no una cláusula completa).
Explica con precisión que "Therefore" pertenece a la familia EFFECT (introduce el resultado) mientras que el cambio al rate-limiter funciona como la CAUSA en esta oración, por lo que la dirección del conector debía invertirse.

Writing Task Instructions

You will write a paragraph explaining the causes and consequences of a given phenomenon. You must use connectors from both families — cause and effect — and demonstrate awareness of register and grammatical form. Target length: 110–140 words. Allow approximately 10 minutes. Your writing will be tracked for academic integrity purposes.

Topic: "The relationship between social media use and declining attention spans in young adults."

Write a paragraph (110–140 words) that explains both the causes and consequences of this relationship. Your paragraph must include:

— At least two cause connectors (because, since, as, due to, owing to)
— At least two effect connectors (therefore, consequently, thus, hence, as a result)
— Appropriate register throughout (no so as a connector)
— Correct grammar after due to or owing to (noun phrase only)
