Classification

A classification divides a concept into categories and answers the reader’s question: what types are there? Its signature is a verb that announces the pattern and an enumeration that makes the limits of each category visible.

The model

This sentence comes from an open-access article about translation and interpreting training. Find the verb that announces the pattern.

The International Association of Conference Interpreters (AIIC) classifies interpreters’ working languages as A (native/active), B (non-native but near-native, also active), and C (passive, used for comprehension only).
Lozano-Argüelles, C., & Martínez-Gómez, A. (2025). Language learning bridge: How translation and interpreting training impacts bilingual proficiency in heritage and L2 learners. Frontiers in Education, 10, Article 1609736. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2025.1609736

Taking it apart

Label each segment: Classifier (announces the pattern) or Category.

The International Association of Conference Interpreters (AIIC) classifies interpreters’ working languages as
A (native/active),
B (non-native but near-native, also active),
and C (passive, used for comprehension only).
«La enumeración hace visibles los límites de cada categoría; cada letra recibe su propia definición entre paréntesis.»

Rebuild it: apply the scheme

Using the AIIC scheme above, place each description in the right category.

The interpreter’s native language, which they use actively
A non-native language mastered at near-native level and used actively
A language the interpreter only understands, without speaking it in the booth
A language used only for comprehension

Rebuild it: complete the classification

Complete the key word of the classification.

The AIIC ___ interpreters’ working languages as A, B, and C.

C languages are passive and used for ___ only.

B languages are non-native but near-native, and are also ___.

Is it a classification?

Which sentence is a classification and not a cause-effect or process sentence?

Sediment contamination rose because wastewater was discharged into the estuary.
Heavy metals can be classified into three groups according to their toxicity.
Samples were first collected, then filtered and finally analysed.
Respuesta: B («can be classified into…»). A es causa-efecto («because»); C es proceso («first… then… finally»).

Phrase bank: classification

Save the phrases you want to reuse. They go to your personal notebook.

X can be classified into three types. X is divided into A, B, and C. One way to categorize X is by… [Field] classifies [concept] as A, B, and C.

Now you write

You will write your own paragraph using the Classification pattern. Allow about 10 minutes. Your writing is tracked for academic integrity: write it yourself, in your own words.

Write in English a short classification paragraph of three to five sentences about a concept from your field. Name the criterion you use to divide it and define each category.

Self-assessment rubric: Classification

Before you submit, check your paragraph against each row. You can ask the AI for a quick check (maximum two times): it suggests the marks and you decide. If three or more rows need work, rewrite before you hand it in.

CriterionAchievedNeeds work
This pattern
I chose one pattern; I did not mix classification with cause and effect in the same paragraph.
I use at least one signal typical of classification (classified as, divided into, types, categories).
The categories are clearly distinguished and none is missing from the scheme.
A reader who reads only my first sentence can anticipate that a classification follows.
General writing criteria
Academic register: formal tone, no contractions or colloquial expressions.
Vocabulary: precise, specific words instead of vague ones (thing, stuff, a lot of).
Cohesion: connectors and reference words link each sentence to the previous one.
Grammar and mechanics: complete sentences, agreement, punctuation, spelling.
Focus: the whole paragraph develops one main idea.
Error frecuente: categorías que se solapan o falta una que completa el esquema.
