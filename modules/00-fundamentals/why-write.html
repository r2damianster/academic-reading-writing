Identifying Your Audience

Writing is always communicative. Every text has a sender, a message, and a receiver.

To deliver a successful argument, you must identify your target reader and understand what they already know about the topic.

Quick Check

In an academic context, what is the primary nature of the 'message' being delivered?

A. A collection of personal feelings
B. A fact-based argument
C. An informal update
Respuesta: B — A fact-based argument.
Tip: Recordar a los estudiantes que el objetivo académico es informar y persuadir mediante evidencia, no simplemente expresar gustos.

The Rule of Objectivity

Academic writing must be objective. This means avoiding biased language and keeping your personal opinions separate from the evidence.

Your goal is to shed light on the topic through analysis, not to present a subjective statement.

Apply It: Rewrite for an Academic Audience

Paragraph draft (student submission):

"I honestly think social media is ruining kids' lives these days, and it's super obvious that schools should just ban phones completely. Everyone knows this is true, and anyone who disagrees just isn't paying attention."

This paragraph fails as academic writing for two reasons: its message is subjective opinion rather than a fact-based argument, and its register is informal rather than formal. Rewrite it in 2-3 sentences as an academic-register paragraph, replacing the personal-opinion markers with an objective, evidence-oriented claim. Then, in one sentence, explain specifically which words or phrases made the original subjective rather than objective.

La reescritura elimina los marcadores de opinión personal ("I honestly think", "it's super obvious", "everyone knows", "anyone who disagrees isn't paying attention") y reformula la afirmación como un enunciado objetivo orientado a evidencia, en registro formal (sin frases como "kids' lives these days").
La explicación identifica con precisión qué palabras o frases específicas del original la hacían subjetiva (p. ej. "I honestly think", "everyone knows"), en contraste con lo que exige una afirmación académica objetiva (basada en evidencia, no en certeza personal).

Register and Style

Academic writing follows a strict formal register. Using the wrong style is often compared to wearing inappropriate clothing for a formal event.

What is effective in a text message or a blog post is usually not suitable for a university essay or a research paper.

Adaptability

How should a student determine the appropriate 'dress code' (style) for their writing?

A. By using the most complex words possible.
B. By studying texts and journals within their specific discipline.
C. By following the same style for every subject.
Respuesta: B — By studying texts and journals within their specific discipline.
Tip: Comparar la escritura académica con un "uniforme" que cambia según la profesión (médicos vs. abogados).

Disciplinary Variation

While the basics of clarity remain, structures vary by field:

An article in Physics may use different referencing than one in History.

Final Review

Which of the following is essential for successful university-level writing?

A. Including as much personal bias as possible.
B. Ignoring the specific conventions of the discipline.
C. Maintaining an objective and formal tone.
Respuesta: C — Maintaining an objective and formal tone.
Tip: Resaltar que la objetividad no significa falta de opinión, sino que la opinión debe estar respaldada por datos.
