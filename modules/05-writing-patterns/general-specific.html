General to Specific

Academic texts that introduce a topic start far away and end up close. The first sentence situates the reader in a broad field; the next ones narrow the focus until they reach the precise point the text needs to raise. This general → specific movement is the most basic pattern of academic writing.

Objetivo: movimiento de lo general a lo específico (introducciones, definiciones, marcos teóricos). Modelo: Milgram (1963). Prepara CARS (Cap. 13), que es este patrón a escala de sección. Solapa con «Organizational Patterns» de Fundamentals.

The model

In 1963 Stanley Milgram published one of the most cited articles in the history of psychology. Its introduction opens with three sentences that visibly narrow the field.

Obedience is the psychological mechanism that links individual action to political purpose. It is the dispositional cement that binds men to systems of authority. Facts of recent history and observation in daily life suggest that for many people obedience may be a deeply ingrained behavior tendency, indeed, a prepotent impulse overriding training in ethics, sympathy, and moral conduct.
Milgram, S. (1963). Behavioral study of obedience. Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology, 67(4), 371–378. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0040525
Fuente real (Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology). El artículo continúa estrechando hasta la pregunta experimental exacta; el patrón ya está completo en estas tres oraciones.

Taking it apart

At what level of generality does each sentence work?

Obedience is the psychological mechanism that links individual action to political purpose.
It is the dispositional cement that binds men to systems of authority.
Facts of recent history and observation in daily life suggest that for many people obedience may be a deeply ingrained behavior tendency, indeed, a prepotent impulse overriding training in ethics, sympathy, and moral conduct.
Clave: General → Intermedio → Específico (Figura 3.1 del libro).

Why this works: a sustained descent

In semantic-wave terms, general → specific is one long descent: the first sentence has maximum abstraction and no data; the last one reverses the proportion.

Obedience is the psychological mechanism that links individual action to political purpose.
It is the dispositional cement that binds men to systems of authority.
Facts of recent history and observation in daily life suggest…
«Establece el terreno antes de sembrar»: por eso es el movimiento por defecto de introducciones y definiciones. Si empieza con un dato muy específico sin preparación, el lector no tiene marco donde ubicarlo.

Spot the pattern: a second text

This is the introduction of a 2023 open-access study. Label the level of each sentence.

Bilingual advantages in learning novel languages have been attributed to both (i) direct transfer of previous language knowledge and skills and (ii) indirect effects of bilingualism, such as enhanced cognitive and metalinguistic skills (Hirosh and Degani, 2018).
Even though the literature on bilingual FL learning advantages is growing rapidly, evidence so far is limited to pupils with typical language development and it is unclear whether such advantages also extend to bilingual children with learning disabilities.
This paper aims to address this gap by comparing English as a foreign language (EFL) skills of monolingual and bilingual children with developmental language disorder (DLD) attending special education schools in the Netherlands.
Tribushinina, E., & Mackaaij, M. (2023). Bilingual advantages in foreign language learning: Evidence from primary-school pupils with developmental language disorder. Frontiers in Education, 8, Article 1264120. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2023.1264120
Mismo texto reaparece como CARS (Cap. 13): «el patrón general a específico a escala de sección completa».

Rebuild it: restore the funnel

These five sentences belong to the introduction of an article about water pollution. Drag them into general → specific order.

Water pollution is one of the most pressing environmental challenges in developing countries.
Coastal cities that depend on both fishing and tourism face a particular tension between economic productivity and environmental quality.
In Ecuador, industrial and domestic wastewater treatment remains uneven, with coverage gaps concentrated in smaller coastal municipalities.
In the municipality of Manta, untreated wastewater from the tuna processing industry is discharged directly into the estuary of the Río Muerto.
The present study analyzes the concentration of heavy metals in sediment samples collected at five points along the estuary between January and June 2024.
Orden (Apéndice C): (b), (c), (e), (a), (d): agua en general → ciudades costeras → Ecuador → Manta → el estudio puntual.

Rebuild it: find the jump

A student’s introduction goes from “Education is important in modern society.” straight to “This study measures vocabulary gains in 12 learners in one Manta school in March 2024.” What is the problem?

The first sentence is too specific.
The text jumps from a very general level to a very specific one with no intermediate step, so the reader notices the leap.
The second sentence should come first.
Respuesta: B. Pregunta del libro: «¿Hay alguna oración que salte un nivel…? ¿el lector nota el salto?». Solución: añadir una o dos oraciones de nivel intermedio (vocabulario en L2, estudiantes ecuatorianos…).

Spot it in the wild

Find the introduction of an empirical article from your field, published in open access. Read the first three or four sentences.

  1. Which is the most general sentence? What broad field does it set up?
  2. In which sentence is the specific topic of the article first mentioned?
  3. Is there a sentence that skips a level, going from very general to very specific without a transition? Does the reader notice the jump?

Phrase bank: general to specific

Save the phrases you want to reuse. They go to your personal notebook.

[Field/Topic] has received increasing attention in recent decades. [Topic] is a widely recognized challenge in [discipline/context]. Research on [broad area] has grown substantially since [reference point]. [Topic] remains one of the central concerns in [field].
Within this broader context, [narrower topic] has emerged as a key concern. A particularly relevant aspect of [broad topic] is [specific aspect]. Among these factors, [specific factor] has attracted particular interest. In [specific region/context], this issue takes on additional significance.
Despite this growth, [specific gap] remains underexplored. The present study addresses this gap by [specific action]. This paper examines [specific object] in the context of [specific setting]. The aim of this study is to [specific verb + object].

Now you write

You will write your own paragraph using the General → Specific pattern. Allow about 10 minutes. Your writing is tracked for academic integrity: write it yourself, in your own words.

Write in English the introduction to a topic from your field, three to five sentences. The first sentence must be the most general; the last, the most specific.

Self-assessment rubric: General → Specific

Before you submit, check your paragraph against each row. You can ask the AI for a quick check (maximum two times): it suggests the marks and you decide. If three or more rows need work, rewrite before you hand it in.

CriterionAchievedNeeds work
This pattern
My first sentence places the reader in the broad field, without mentioning my particular topic.
Each following sentence is more specific than the previous one.
My last sentence names the exact topic or question.
A reader who does not know my field can follow the thread without skipping a sentence.
General writing criteria
Academic register: formal tone, no contractions or colloquial expressions.
Vocabulary: precise, specific words instead of vague ones (thing, stuff, a lot of).
Cohesion: connectors and reference words link each sentence to the previous one.
Grammar and mechanics: complete sentences, agreement, punctuation, spelling.
Focus: the whole paragraph develops one main idea.
