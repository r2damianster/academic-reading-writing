The P.E.E.R. Framework

What is it?

The P.E.E.R. structure is a method for organizing paragraphs to ensure every argument is solid, supported, and connected to your thesis.

This framework is essential for building a strong Body Paragraph that guides the reader through your logic.

The Point

What is the primary function of the 'Point' at the start?

A. To list bibliographic sources.
B. To present the main Topic Sentence you will defend.
Respuesta: B.
Tip: El Point es la brújula. Si el primer enunciado no es claro, el lector se perderá en la evidencia que sigue.

The Evidence

In the 'Evidence' phase, what information is most appropriate?

A. Direct quotes, statistics, or data from reliable sources.
B. Personal opinions based on general feelings.
Respuesta: A.
Tip: En la academia, "Evidence" significa algo que alguien más puede verificar. Las opiniones personales ("yo siento que...") no cuentan como evidencia.

Deepening: Explanation

The most critical step is the Explanation. It is not enough to simply provide a quote; you must explain how that evidence proves your initial point.

Quick Check

If a student provides a statistic but fails to analyze it, which part is missing?

A. Point
B. Explanation
Respuesta: B.
Tip: Este es el error más común. Los alumnos "sueltan" el dato y asumen que el lector entenderá la conexión. La Explanation es donde tú explicas qué significa ese dato.

Deepening: Relevance

The final step, Relevance (Link), connects the paragraph back to the thesis or acts as a bridge. It answers: "Why does this matter?"

Apply It: Rebalance the P.E.E.R. Paragraph

Paragraph draft (student submission):

"Homeschooling can offer children more personalized attention than traditional schooling. According to a 2021 NHERI study, 87% of homeschooling parents cited 'individualized instruction' as their primary motivation. A separate survey by Ray (2020) found that homeschooled students scored, on average, in the 87th percentile on standardized tests, compared to the 50th percentile for public school students. Coleman (2019) also notes that homeschool co-ops have grown by 25% since 2015."

This paragraph is almost entirely Evidence with no Explanation or Relevance — a classic Evidence Dump. Write 1-2 Explanation sentences that connect ONE specific statistic above back to the Point (personalized attention), plus one closing Relevance sentence that links the evidence back to why it matters for the broader argument about homeschooling.

La(s) oración(es) de Explanation interpretan específicamente una de las estadísticas dadas (el 87% del estudio NHERI o el percentil 87 de Ray) y la conectan explícitamente con el Point sobre atención personalizada, en lugar de solo repetir el dato.
La oración de Relevance/Link explica por qué esta evidencia importa para el argumento más amplio sobre el homeschooling (p. ej. implicaciones para la elección educativa de las familias), sin introducir un tema nuevo no relacionado.

Mastered?

What is the risk of omitting the 'R' (Relevance) at the end?

A. The teacher might think the quote is fake.
B. The paragraph will look like a list of isolated facts without a connection to the thesis.
Respuesta: B.
Tip: La Relevance (o Link) cierra la puerta. Le recuerda al lector cómo este párrafo ayuda a probar la tesis general del ensayo.
