Diversity in Rhetorical Patterns

In academic writing, your choice of essay type is dictated by your communicative purpose. We will explore the specialized patterns: Dialectical, Exemplification, Narrative/Descriptive, and History Thesis.

Common Universal Structure: Regardless of the type, every essay MUST contain an Introduction (with thesis), Body (with topic/supporting sentences), and a Conclusion.

The Dialectical Essay

This type is fundamental for critical thinking. Its purpose is to analyze complex events from different positions, showing respect for opposing points of view to reach a resolution.
