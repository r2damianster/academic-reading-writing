Concession and Counterargument

The difference between a competent argument and a strong one is often concession: acknowledging the merit of an objection or limitation before reaffirming your own position. Far from weakening the argument, it strengthens it, because it shows you considered the alternatives and still hold your ground. Carl Rogers called this non-adversarial argument; today it is known as a Rogerian move.

The model

This paragraph comes from the limitations section of an open-access article on translation and interpreting training. It shows concession in its most honest form: the author admits an objection to their own study before refuting it. Look for the exact pivot.

This study has two primary limitations that warrant discussion. First, the absence of a control group constrains our ability to isolate the effects of the program from other potential influences on language development. One might hypothesize that linguistic improvement occurs simply through immersion in an academic environment, which inherently demands Superior-level linguistic functions such as presenting opinions, using specialized vocabulary, formulating hypotheses, and engaging with abstract topics. However, this hypothesis is challenged by existing research on comparable populations, which indicates that nearly 70% of students in heritage learner courses fail to demonstrate improvements in written proficiency (Gatti et al., 2024).
Lozano-Argüelles, C., & Martínez-Gómez, A. (2025). Language learning bridge: How translation and interpreting training impacts bilingual proficiency in heritage and L2 learners. Frontiers in Education, 10, Article 1609736. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2025.1609736

Taking it apart

Label each step of the concession “ladder”.

This study has two primary limitations that warrant discussion. First, the absence of a control group constrains our ability to isolate the effects of the program from other potential influences on language development.
One might hypothesize that linguistic improvement occurs simply through immersion in an academic environment, which inherently demands Superior-level linguistic functions…
However, this hypothesis is challenged by existing research on comparable populations,
which indicates that nearly 70% of students in heritage learner courses fail to demonstrate improvements in written proficiency (Gatti et al., 2024).
«Escalera de tres peldaños»: reconocer la limitación, formular la objeción más seria en su versión más fuerte y refutarla con evidencia.

Why concession works

The academic reader distrusts absolute positions. An author who admits no objection seems naive or dishonest; one who admits the strongest ones and still holds their position seems rigorous.

In Toulmin’s terms, concession is an anticipated Rebuttal: you identify the conditions under which your Claim might fail and integrate them before the reader raises them.

Carl Rogers proposed that the most effective way to persuade someone who disagrees is first to show that you understood them. A Rogerian move does not attack the opposing view; it restates it precisely, acknowledges what is valid, and then shows why your position covers wider ground. The reader feels included, not fought.

Pregunta de discusión: ¿una concesión fácil de refutar («straw man») sigue siendo rogeriana? No: la objeción debe ser real.

Rebuild it: add the concession

“Online language learning platforms provide learners with flexible scheduling and access to native speaker tutors regardless of geographic location. A meta-analysis of 45 studies found that students using synchronous online tutoring achieved proficiency gains comparable to those in face-to-face instruction (Means et al., 2013). These platforms should therefore be adopted as the primary mode of language instruction in universities with limited faculty resources.”

This paragraph states a position and gives evidence, but it has no concession, so it is vulnerable. Write one concession sentence that acknowledges the most obvious objection and one reaffirmation sentence that gives strength back to the original position. Say where you would insert them.

The concession acknowledges a real, strong objection (for example, that online platforms cannot replicate spontaneous face-to-face interaction) using a visible concessive structure.
The reaffirmation returns to the original position with a reason or evidence (for example, the flexibility benefit for adult learners) and the answer indicates where both sentences would be inserted in the paragraph.
Modelo (Apéndice C): «It could be argued that online platforms simply cannot replicate the spontaneous, embodied interaction of a physical classroom, and for some learners this loss is real. However, for adult learners balancing work and study, the flexibility of asynchronous access outweighs this limitation in practice.»

Spot it in the wild

Find a discussion paragraph in an article from your field where the author acknowledges a limitation of their own study before reasserting the validity of their results.

  1. Does the author acknowledge the objection with a concessive structure (although, while, even though, it is true that), or simply mention it without accepting it?
  2. Is the reaffirmation that follows stronger or weaker than the initial position? What effect does that difference have?
  3. Does the author offer a synthesis that goes beyond the opposition, or keep both positions separate?

Not graded. Bring your answers to class and compare them with a classmate.

Now you write

You will write your own paragraph using the Concession and Rogerian moves pattern. Allow about 15 minutes. Your writing is tracked for academic integrity: write it yourself, in your own words.

Choose a topic from your field on which you hold a firm position. Write in English a paragraph of five to seven sentences that includes at least one concession and one reaffirmation. Follow the structure: position → concession → reaffirmation (→ synthesis, if you have one).

This pattern
My concession acknowledges a real objection, not an invented one that is easy to refute.
I use a visible concessive structure (although, while, it is true that).
My reaffirmation is stronger after the concession than before it.
The tone is academic: I do not dismiss the opposing position, I move beyond it.
If I include a synthesis, it offers a position that integrates the best of both sides.
General writing criteria
Academic register: formal tone, no contractions or colloquial expressions.
Vocabulary: precise, specific words instead of vague ones (thing, stuff, a lot of).
Cohesion: connectors and reference words link each sentence to the previous one.
Grammar and mechanics: complete sentences, agreement, punctuation, spelling.
Focus: the whole paragraph develops one main idea.
Certainty: hedges and boosters match the strength of the evidence.
Error frecuente: concesión «de paja» (objeción inventada, fácil de refutar).
